Where the words went

Twelve weeks of readings, grouped here into eleven threads, plus the introductions. I gave the most words to the podcast on Casap and Hunter’s World Peace Game — and most of those were replies, not the post. Perkins got a post and no conversation at all. Mishra drew seven replies to one post; the intersection of pedagogy, content and technology was the thing I kept coming back to in other people’s threads.

The register

Six measurements from the voice profile, with the discussion board placed among the registers already measured: three academic papers and three books. Not one em dash in 9,266 words — the same switch-off the Doane study showed, in a form nobody was grading for style. The comments carry more first person per ten thousand words than any of the three papers and two of the three books; the posts sit with the papers. Ten spaced hyphens did the dash’s job.
