UNL PhD Coursework

Fall 2022 – Summer 2026

Program record

Fifteen courses toward a PhD in Educational Studies, with a specialization in Innovative Learning Technologies and a graduate certificate in Mixed Methods Research. They are grouped below by strand — methods, theory, design, practice, seminar — rather than by term.

Grouping coursework thematically is a retrospective act. The registrar handed these out in whatever order they were offered; the strands are something I imposed afterward, and they hold together better in some places than others. Five courses have artifact pages. The rest are archived coursework — weekly writing, annotated bibliographies, stance statements, final projects — and are listed here because a record that only shows the finished parts is not a record.

Some of this is also dissertation data. The technology autobiography from Fall 2022 is flagged below because it was written in my first term, when I still thought the dissertation would be about gamification in a maths classroom — which is exactly what makes it usable. It was not written for the argument it now serves. Realistically most of what is on this page is data; the flag marks where that is deliberate rather than merely true.

Learning Technologies & Design

The specialization. A survey, then mathematics, then two passes at AI and APIs three years apart, then the two production courses where things actually had to be built.

    First course in the program. A technology autobiography, a school technology survey with its own dataset, and a technology portfolio. The autobiography predates the dissertation by three years and does not know it is evidence.
