TEAC 949B

Critical, Anti-colonial, and Decolonizing Theories in Education

University of Nebraska–Lincoln | 3 credit hours | Artifacts online

What this course was

Freire, Tuck and Yang, Anzaldúa, Delgado Bernal, Pérez Huber, Tuhiwai Smith, Malcolm X. The effect of reading them together, in one term, was mostly anger. I wrote that down at the time, in the second essential learning reflection, and there is no reason to soften it now: where were these frameworks when I was a teacher candidate?

Tuck and Yang set the terms. Decolonization is not a metaphor — when metaphor invades decolonization it recenters whiteness, resettles theory, and extends innocence to the settler. Scheurich and Young made the same charge against epistemology itself, arguing that the ways of knowing Western institutions revere are racist at the civilizational level, and that naming a new race-based epistemology does not by itself dislodge the old one. Neither position lets a white educator off. Neither is meant to.

Two things stuck. Youth Participatory Action Research, which named something I had been half-doing for years without a word for it. And Chicana feminist epistemology, which offered a way to treat religion, family and inherited knowledge as sources rather than as things to be gotten over. The class gave me vocabulary I did not know I lacked.

The final paper is a YPAR proposal for de/colonizing a mathematics classroom, and it opens with an “I am” poem because the epistemology it draws on does not permit the author to stand outside the work. Reading it now, the argument I thought I was making about somebody else’s classroom is the argument the dissertation makes about my own. I did not notice that at the time.

Through line

Two things in this term’s work belong to the dissertation, and neither was recognized as belonging to it at the time.

The final paper records the moment I understood that my mother’s homeschooling had been a mild form of Youth Participatory Action Research — that the thing I was proposing to do in somebody else’s classroom had already been done to me. Reading one’s own education as data rather than as background is the dissertation’s central move, and it is written down here, in Fall 2023. The study was not reframed as a mixed methods autoethnography until Spring 2026. The record dates the turn to the reframe. The paper dates it two and a half years earlier.

The second is smaller and stranger. The same three epigraphs open this paper and the Technology Statement written for TEAC 880E the following spring — Kovalevskaya, Einstein and Weierstrass, all three insisting that a mathematician who is not also a poet is an incomplete mathematician. I did not plan the repetition. I did not notice it until the coursework was assembled into a portfolio and the two documents sat next to each other.

This is what the portfolio is for. A through line one can name is an argument. A through line that turns up in the work before there is language for it is evidence.
