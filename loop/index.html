You Belong in the Loop

Building Agentic AI Workflows Inside Real Constraints

About this session

Most AI sessions hand you prompts that will be obsolete by the time you get back to your desk. This one is about architecture instead: how to audit your own work, identify what is genuinely delegable, and build something that runs without you having to babysit it — inside real constraints, where you cannot paste client data into a chatbot and the network is watching. One rule, one method, and a page you do not have to photograph.

Presented by Sam Seim (they/them) at the 2027 Nebraska Women in STEM Conference, Cornhusker Marriott, Lincoln, March 2–3, 2027.
